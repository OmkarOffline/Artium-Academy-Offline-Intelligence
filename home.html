Average:

The monthly figure averaged over the completed months picked above (3, 6 or 12 months, or every month since the centre opened). The running month is never part of the average. For all centres together, it is the average per centre per month: each centre's own monthly average, worked out only over the months that centre has been open, and then averaged across the centres. So a newly opened centre is never spread over months before it existed. For renewals, a centre counts from the month its first memberships came up for renewal, and on All centres it is added only once it has 6 months of renewals behind it. CM and RR are then also shown per centre, so they compare like for like. Percentages and ATVs are worked out over the whole period, not averaged month by month.

CM (current month):

The figure for the running month so far.

RR (run-rate):

Where the current month will end if it carries on at the same daily rate: the figure so far ÷ days gone × days in the month. The green or red tag compares the run-rate with the average. Renewals due are known for the whole month in advance, so they have no run-rate.

Marketing spends and leads:

Daily Meta and Google spends and leads from the offline marketing tracker. A campaign that names a centre counts for that centre; Chennai campaigns that don't name one are split equally between Alwarpet and Thoraipakkam. Google leads count only the lead-search campaigns; the Local Store campaigns' spend is included, but their store actions (calls, directions, visits) aren't counted as leads.

CPL (cost per lead):

Marketing spend on a channel ÷ leads from that channel. Blended CPL uses the spend and leads of all channels together.

CAC (customer acquisition cost):

Total marketing spend ÷ conversions (admissions), over the months spends are recorded for.

Cost per appointment / cost per centre visit:

Total marketing spend ÷ appointments booked, or ÷ centre visits.

Appointments:

Appointments placed in the month they were expected to visit. In the current month, only appointments up to today are counted.

Centre visits:

Every visit logged at the centre, scheduled or walk-in.

Appointments to visits %:

Centre visits ÷ appointments in the same months. Walk-ins are included, so it can go above 100%.

Conversions:

New admissions (Fresh Sales) in the month.

Visits to conversions %:

Admissions (Fresh Sales) ÷ centre visits in the same months.

Time to conversion:

Days from a learner's centre visit to their admission, averaged over the admissions in the month. Lower is better, so a faster CM shows green.

ATV (average transaction value):

Money collected ÷ number of transactions: admissions for Fresh Sales, renewals for Renewals.

Renewals due:

Memberships whose end date falls in the month. First-time renewals are due from an admission; repeated renewals are due from an earlier renewal.

Successful renewals:

Renewals actually paid in the month, whether they were due that month, earlier or later. Each is first-time or repeated depending on whether the learner had renewed before. First-time and repeated add up to the overall figure.

Renewal collection efficiency:

Successful renewals ÷ renewals due, the same way as on Monthly Performance Analysis.

Not connected yet:

Costs and CPLs:
