Memberships ended:

Admissions whose membership ended yesterday or earlier. Memberships still running aren't due for renewal yet, so they're left out.

First renewal:

A learner's first renewal after joining. Learners who haven't renewed are left out of the day counts.

To 1st class:

Average days from the day a learner joined to the day their membership started, for the learners who renewed.

Admission → first renewal:

Days from the day a learner joined to their first renewal. It is made up of To 1st class, the package itself, and the days taken to renew after the membership ends.

Renewal → next renewal:

For learners who have renewed two or more times, the days between each renewal and the next. Every gap counts, so someone with 20 renewals adds 19 gaps. Each gap sits in the row of the package bought at the earlier renewal.

Average and median:

The average adds everyone's days and divides by the number of learners, so a few very late renewals pull it up. The median is the middle learner when everyone is lined up from quickest to slowest: half renewed sooner, half later.

Course Subscription by Age Group
—
Admissions for every course, split by the learner's age group.
#:

The number of learners who joined each course in each age group.

%C (share of the column):

Each course's share of an age group's admissions, so every column adds up to 100%. The Total column shows each course's share of all admissions.

%R (share of the row):

Each age group's share of a course's admissions, so every row adds up to 100%. The Total row shows each age group's share of all admissions.

Gold underline:

Marks the highest number. In # and %R it compares the age groups within each course; in %C it compares the courses within each age group. If two numbers are equal, both are underlined.

Renewal Rate by Age Group
—
Out of the memberships that fell due in the chosen period (up to yesterday), the share that renewed — for every course and age group.
Renewal rate:

Memberships that renewed ÷ memberships that were due. A membership is due once it has ended — yesterday or earlier. Hover over a percentage to see the actual numbers.

Period:

Picks memberships by the month they ended. CM is the current month so far; 3M is this month and the two before it; 6M, 9M and 12M work the same way; Lifetime is everything. Pick a month to see just that month. A membership counts as renewed whenever the learner renewed, even if that was after the period, so the most recent months can look lower while renewals are still coming in.

First-time:

A learner's first membership, bought when they joined. It counts as renewed if the learner renewed at any point after joining.

Repeated:

A membership the learner had already renewed into. It counts as renewed if the learner renewed again after it.

Total:

First-time and repeated memberships together.

Teacher filter:

Shows only the memberships taught by that teacher, and only the courses they teach. Under each percentage, ▲ or ▼ shows how far the teacher is above or below everyone teaching the same course and age group, in percentage points. Hover over a cell to see both sets of numbers.

Gold underline:

The age group with the highest renewal rate for each course. Any figure based on fewer than 5 memberships is greyed out (and never underlined), since a handful of learners can swing the percentage.
