Public holidays:

The state government holidays for each city — Chennai (Tamil Nadu), Bangalore (Karnataka) and Mumbai (Maharashtra) — kept in the State Holidays sheet. Small letters show where each one applies: C Chennai, B Bangalore, M Mumbai. The same festival can fall on different dates in different cities. For a year that isn't in the sheet yet, Google's national public holidays are shown for every city. Hover over a holiday to see each city's name for it.

Festivals & special days:

Festivals and observances from the same Google Calendar — useful for planning communication, even when they aren't days off. Days marked ✦ are our own special days for social media planning; the social media team gets an email about each one 20 days before.

Long weekend:

Three or more days off in a row, counting Saturdays, Sundays and that city's public holidays — for example a holiday on a Friday or a Monday. A holiday on a Saturday or Sunday doesn't make one. The city letters show where the long weekend applies; pick a city to see only its long weekends.

Long weekend with 1 day's leave:

A holiday on a Tuesday or Thursday leaves one working day between it and the weekend. Many people take that day off, turning it into a four-day break. The day to take off is marked with a dashed box.

Moved holidays:

If an academy observes a holiday on a different day, the Head of Offline Operations or a Centre Manager can click the holiday and move it — or mark it not observed — for their city. Only that city changes; the other cities keep the original date. A moved holiday shows ↪ on its new date, and the original date shows it crossed out. Long weekends update to match.

Our entries:

Birthdays, school holidays, school exams and other events added by the offline staff. Everyone who can open the calendar sees them; only staff (not teachers) can add, edit or delete them. Click an entry to edit it.

Mondays:

Shaded, since the academy is closed on Mondays.

Refresh:

The calendar checks Google every few hours by itself. Refresh checks again straight away, in case a holiday has been added or moved.
