Admissions:

New learners who joined in the month, from the Fresh Sales sheet, counted under the course they joined. A learner who joins two courses counts once in each.

Filters:

Course level, age group and gender each take as many choices as you like. Shares are worked out within what the filters leave in, so each month still adds up to 100%.

Share of the month:

A course's admissions as a part of all admissions that month. The courses in a month add up to 100%.

Lifetime:

All admissions since the centre opened, and each course's share of them.

Course not mentioned:

Admissions with no course filled in. They still count in the totals.
