Active learners by month
—
Opening balance:

Last month's Monthly Active Learners.

New admissions:

Learners who joined during the month.

Due for renewal:

Memberships that end during the month, counted only up to today. First-time renewals are learners finishing their first membership; repeated renewals are learners finishing a membership they had already renewed.

Actually renewed:

Every renewal made during the month.

Monthly Active Learners:

Total, minus the memberships due for renewal, plus the learners who actually renewed.

Net gain / loss:

This month's Monthly Active Learners minus last month's.

Win-back / Churn:

Learners who actually renewed minus the memberships due for renewal. + means more learners renewed than were due (win-back); − means learners were lost (churn).

Total capacity:

The most learners the centre can take: Alwarpet 540, Thoraipakkam 630, Borewell Road 720. For all centres, it adds up the centres that were open that month.

Capacity utilisation %:

Monthly Active Learners ÷ Total capacity. Red is below 40%, amber 40–55%, green 55–70%, and blue 70% or more.

Admission Requirement
—
No. of teachers:

Teachers at the centre for that course. South Vocals teachers take PFM - Tamil and Carnatic Classical; North Vocals teachers take PFM - Hindi and Hindustani Classical.

Allocation %:

Share of a teacher's time for the course: PFM - Tamil 80% / Carnatic Classical 20%, PFM - Hindi 80% / Hindustani Classical 20%, every other course 100%.

Capacity:

90 learners × No. of teachers × Allocation %.

Target:

70% of capacity.

Monthly Active Learners:

This month's active learners in the course, worked out as in the section above.

% Active utilisation:

Monthly Active Learners ÷ capacity.

Surplus / Deficit:

Target − Monthly Active Learners. A positive number is the admissions still needed; zero or below means no new admissions are needed.

Monthly Active Learners by Teacher
—
Monthly Active Learners:

Worked out exactly as in the first table, but only for that teacher. The Total row matches Monthly Active Learners in the first table.

Not mentioned:

Admissions or renewals with no teacher filled in. They still count in the totals.

Current and past teachers:

Current teachers have active learners this month and an admission or renewal in the last 3 months; they're listed first, biggest first. Past teachers are listed below, with the month of their last admission or renewal. A past teacher can still show a learner or two while a long membership runs out.

Active months:

A teacher's numbers start in the month of their first admission or renewal and stop after the month their last membership ends — the last month a learner of theirs can still be active. Every other month shows 0.

Outside a teacher's active months:

Learners still counted against a teacher outside those months — usually a membership with no end date. They're kept here so the Total still matches the first table.
