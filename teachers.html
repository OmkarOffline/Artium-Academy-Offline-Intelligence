Monthly Active Learners by Teacher
—
Monthly Active Learners:

Worked out exactly as on the Monthly Active Learners page, but only for that teacher. The Total row matches Monthly Active Learners there.

Not mentioned:

Admissions or renewals with no teacher filled in. They still count in the totals.

Current and past teachers:

Current teachers have active learners this month and an admission or renewal in the last 3 months; they're listed first, biggest first. Past teachers are listed below, with the month of their last admission or renewal.

Active months:

A teacher's numbers start in the month of their first admission or renewal and stop after the month their last membership ends. Every other month shows 0.

Outside a teacher's active months:

Learners still counted against a teacher outside those months — usually a membership with no end date. They're kept here so the Total still matches.

Cohort Renewals
—
Cohort:

Every learner whose membership ends in that month, under the teacher who taught that membership.

Renewed:

Learners in the cohort who have renewed so far, whenever they paid — early, on time or late. The figure keeps rising as late payers renew, so older months can still change.

Renewal %:

The share of the cohort that has renewed so far. It tells you how well the teacher held on to that group of learners.

Last 3M, 6M, 12M and Lifetime:

All the cohorts in those months put together: renewed out of due, for completed months only. This month is left out until it's over. Recent cohorts are still renewing, so the last 3 months usually read lower than 12 months — that alone doesn't mean the teacher has slipped.

This year so far:

Memberships ending from January to the end of this month: how many were due, how many have renewed, and how many are still to renew — the learners to reach out to. Those marked "Will Not Renew" are counted in To renew and shown underneath.

Folding and sorting:

The arrow beside each group of summary columns folds it away, and brings it back. Click a summary column's heading to rank teachers by it; click again to go back to the usual order.

This month:

Covers every membership ending this month, including those whose end date hasn't arrived yet.

Renewals Closed in the Month
—
Due:

Memberships ending in that month, under the teacher who taught them.

Renewed:

Every renewal paid in that month — learners due that month, learners due in earlier months who paid late, and learners who paid in advance. Each renewal counts under the teacher of the membership being renewed.

Last 3M, 6M, 12M and Lifetime:

Those months put together: every renewal closed in them out of everything due in them, for completed months only. This month is left out until it's over.

Folding and sorting:

The arrow beside the summary columns folds them away, and brings them back. Click a summary column's heading to rank teachers by it; click again to go back to the usual order.

Renewal %:

Renewed against due for that month. It can go above 100% when late payments and advance renewals come in. The month's figure is final once the month is over. The totals match Renewal collection efficiency on the Renewal Pipeline page.
